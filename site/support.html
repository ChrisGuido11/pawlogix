Support

Short answers. If you need a person, email us.

Not a diagnosis. Always ask your veterinarian. Support here is for using the app, not for medical advice.

Download

PawLogix is on the Apple App Store for iPhone. It is free to download. Download on the App Store.

Scan a record

Open the app, add a pet if you have not already, and photograph a vet invoice, lab printout, or vaccine record. Hold the page flat, fill the frame, and avoid glare. You can also pick a photo you already took. The app produces a plain-English visit summary of that document. It does not diagnose from symptoms.

Ask what a result means

Open a completed record and use chat to ask about something on that paper — a value, an abbreviation, or a line on the invoice. Answers are based on the document you uploaded. They are not a substitute for your vet.

Reminders

You can set reminders for medications, vaccines, and preventives. Allow notifications in iOS Settings if alerts do not appear. Reminders are included with the free download.

Free download and Pro

The free download includes a limited number of scans and follow-up questions, and one pet. PawLogix Pro is $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year, billed by Apple. Pro unlocks more scans, more chat, and more than one pet.

To cancel or change a subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PawLogix. Restore purchases from the paywall screen in the app if you reinstall.

Account

You do not have to create an email account to start. If you add one later, it is for keeping your data. Delete your account in Settings when you want your data removed. Export first if you want a copy.

A summary looks wrong

AI can misread a photo or a word. Compare the summary with the original paper. If something important disagrees, trust the document and your veterinarian — not the app. Try a sharper photo if the scan failed.
