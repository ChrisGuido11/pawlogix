Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Your privacy, and your pet’s health information, matter. This policy explains how PawLogix collects, uses, and protects information when you use the iOS app.

Our commitment

We collect what we need to run the product: store the records you upload, generate a plain-English summary, answer follow-up questions about those records, and send the reminders you set. We do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

Account and pet data

  • You can use PawLogix without creating an email account. If you later add an email and password, we store that email.
  • Pet profiles you enter (name, species, breed, age or date of birth, weight, notes, and an optional photo)
  • Veterinary documents you photograph or select, plus the text and summaries generated from them
  • Reminders and notification preferences you choose

Usage and device data

  • Basic app activity needed to operate the Service (for example, whether a scan succeeded)
  • Device type, operating system, and app version when needed to fix problems
  • A technical identifier for your session so your records stay on your account

Payment information

Subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store. We do not collect or store your payment card number. Apple tells us whether a PawLogix Pro subscription is active so we can unlock Pro features.

How we use information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve PawLogix
  • Generate visit summaries and answers about documents you upload
  • Send the medication, vaccine, and preventive reminders you enable
  • Respond to support requests
  • Manage subscriptions and prevent abuse
  • Comply with law

AI processing

When you scan a record or ask a follow-up question, images and related text are sent to Anthropic (Claude) so we can produce a plain-English summary and answers. That material may include your pet’s health information. Use those outputs as a reading aid, not as a diagnosis.

Storage and security

Pet records and photos are stored so you can open them later. We use cloud infrastructure (including Supabase) and transmit data over HTTPS. Our cloud provider encrypts data at rest. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third parties

  • Anthropic. Powers record interpretation and in-app questions about a record.
  • Supabase. Database, authentication, and file storage for the app.
  • Apple. Processes App Store payments and subscription status.

Those companies have their own privacy policies. We ask them to process data only as needed to provide their service to us.

Your rights

  • Access and correction. Most information is visible and editable in the app.
  • Export. You can export your data from Settings in the app.
  • Delete. Delete your account in Settings, or email us. We will delete associated personal data as described below.

To make a request, email cguido85@icloud.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Retention

We keep your information while your account or anonymous session is active and we need it to provide the Service. After you delete your account, we delete personal data within 30 days, purge backups within 90 days, and may keep a limited record if the law requires it (for example, to resolve a dispute). Anonymized, aggregated information that cannot reasonably identify you may be retained for product improvement.

App permissions

  • Camera. To photograph vet records and pet photos, only when you take a picture.
  • Photo library. To choose an existing photo you select. We do not browse your library.
  • Notifications. For medication, vaccine, and preventive reminders you turn on.
  • Network. To sync records, run AI features, and check subscription status.

We do not request location access. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

Children

PawLogix is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact cguido85@icloud.com and we will delete it. A parent or guardian is responsible if they allow a child 13 or older to use the app.

International transfers

PawLogix is operated from the United States. If you use the Service from another country, your information may be processed in the United States or in other countries where our providers operate. By using PawLogix, you understand that transfer.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the “Last updated” date and, for significant changes, try to notify you in the app or by email if you have one on file. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
