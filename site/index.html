How it works

Three things, on your phone. That is the product.

1

Snap a vet invoice or lab

Take a photo of the paper from the visit — invoice, lab printout, or vaccine record. Keep the page flat and readable.

2

Read a plain-English visit summary

Medical jargon becomes a summary of what the document says, in language meant for a pet parent — not a clinic chart.

3

Ask what a result means

Follow up on that record. Set reminders for medications, vaccines, and preventives so the next dose does not slip.

Warm about your pet. Strict about medicine.

PawLogix is a translator for documents you already brought home. It is not a second opinion that replaces your vet.

What it does

  • Turns a photographed vet invoice or lab into a visit summary you can read.
  • Lets you ask what a value or abbreviation on that record means.
  • Reminds you about medications, vaccines, and preventives you choose to track.
  • Keeps dogs and cats in one place on your iPhone.

What it does not do

  • It does not diagnose illness.
  • It does not prescribe treatment or change a dose.
  • It does not replace a conversation with your veterinarian.
  • It does not guess from symptoms. It reads the document you upload.
